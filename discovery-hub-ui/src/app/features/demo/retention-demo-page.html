Retention & disposition demo

Pushes one message through the real pipeline — ingestion API, Kafka, the worker, MongoDB and S3 — with a retention period of a few minutes, then watches the scheduled disposition job delete it from both stores.

The period applies to this message only. Shortening the period for EMAIL would expire every email already archived, so the next run would take the corpus with it; a per-message period cannot affect anything ingested before it. Capped at {{ maxRetentionMinutes }} minutes by the service.
